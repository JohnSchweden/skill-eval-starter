We stopped writing meeting notes by hand a while ago. The AI notetaker does it now.

Nobody reads them either way.

I used to think the problem was the notes: slow capture, messy summaries, action items that got lost. We fixed all of that, and our meetings didn't get better.

The real problem was meetings that ended without a decision. Everyone talked, everyone nodded, and the question was still open when people left. No transcription tool fixes that.

Now we ask one question before we book a meeting: what decision will this produce? If we can't answer it, we don't meet. We send a message or make the call ourselves.

When a meeting does happen, it ends with the decision stated out loud, with a name next to it. The notes are an afterthought.

Note-taking tools are getting very good. But a perfect record of a decision nobody made is still just paperwork.
