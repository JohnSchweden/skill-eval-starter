We stopped writing meeting notes by hand. An AI notetaker does it now.

Here's the uncomfortable part: nobody reads the notes either way. Handwritten or AI-generated, they pile up in a folder and disappear.

We kept polishing the notes when the real problem was the meetings themselves. Too many of them ended without anyone deciding anything. Someone said "let's circle back," everyone nodded, and we all left with a summary of a conversation that never went anywhere.

So we changed the question we ask before every meeting: what decision are we here to make? If the answer is "none," we cancel it or make it async.

The notetaker is a nice convenience. The decisions are what matter.
