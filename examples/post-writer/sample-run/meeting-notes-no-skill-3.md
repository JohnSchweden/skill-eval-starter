We stopped writing meeting notes by hand. Our AI notetaker does it now.

Nobody reads them either way.

For a while I thought the notes were the problem. They weren't. The problem was meetings that ended without a decision. Someone would say "let's circle back," everyone nodded, and the notes captured a conversation that went nowhere.

Now, before any meeting goes on the calendar, we ask one question: what decision are we making here? If we can't answer it, we don't meet. We send a message instead.

Good notes won't fix a meeting that shouldn't have happened. Clear decisions will.
