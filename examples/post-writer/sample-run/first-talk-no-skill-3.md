Gave my first conference talk yesterday. 25 minutes on stage.

At minute 3, the slides failed. Nothing came up on screen.

I had two choices: stop and wait for the tech to get fixed, or keep talking. I kept talking. I couldn't point to the next slide, so I described the next idea out loud and let the audience picture it.

Afterward, several people told me the part without slides was the best part of the talk.

A few things I took away:

- If you know your material, you can carry a room without visuals.
- Slides support the talk. They don't have to be the talk.
- Technical problems are going to happen. How you recover matters more than avoiding them.

First talks are nerve-wracking. This one taught me more than any amount of rehearsal would have.

If you've had a moment where the plan fell apart on stage, I'd like to hear how you handled it.
